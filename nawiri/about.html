The Name

What "Nawiri" means

"Nawiri" means to bloom. It is a word that carries patience: the understanding that growth does not happen all at once, but in seasons, and with the right conditions. Rooted, gradual, and real. We exist to create those conditions, because mental wellness is not a destination or a cure. It is a living, ongoing process of becoming. For individuals ready to begin. For communities that have been waiting too long.
Who We Are

A consultancy and a community, built together

Together they form one ecosystem: professional excellence and community access, connected by a shared belief that mental wellness belongs to everyone.

Nawiri Akili-Care Africa

A professional mental health consultancy delivering evidence-based therapy, psychological consultation, and organisational wellness rooted in local understanding and global best practice.

Nawiri Akili Initiative

Our community impact arm, extending the mission into schools and communities through education, awareness, and subsidised or free care for those who cannot afford professional support.

Vision · Mission · Approach

What we stand for

Our Vision

A future where mental wellness is understood, accessible, and actively supported across Africa and beyond, where blooming is not the exception but the expectation.

Our Mission

To provide professional, evidence-based mental health care, and to extend that care into communities through education, subsidised or free support, and lasting partnerships, for a mentally flourishing community.

Our Approach

Evidence-based. Community-centered. Culturally grounded. We build care that belongs here, rooted in African reality, informed by global best practice, and committed to access above all else.

Our Model

How We Work: Profit for Purpose

Nawiri operates as a hybrid social enterprise. Nawiri Akili-Care Africa, our registered consultancy, generates professional revenue through therapy and organisational wellness services. That revenue directly funds the Nawiri Akili Initiative, our community impact arm.

Community sponsorships and partnerships are received and managed through the consultancy while the Initiative's formal CBO registration is being finalised. Every contribution is tracked independently to ensure community-first funds go directly toward subsidised care, school programs, and free community sessions.

We believe professional expertise should fuel social change. Thank you for partnering with us to help communities bloom.

Founder

Built from experience. Driven by purpose.

Nawiri was born from a professional understanding, a caring heart, and a mind that has firsthand known the weight of struggling without support.

Peris Gichuki is a licensed psychologist with over five years of clinical and community experience spanning psychotherapy, psychosocial counselling, trauma-informed care, addiction rehabilitation, GBV-integrated support, clinical supervision, and community outreach. She has worked with individuals, families, groups, institutions, and communities across clinical settings, rehabilitation centres, schools, and grassroots programs, bringing the same standard of care to every context and every person she has served.

She was someone who grew up wishing she had the language to name what she felt, the guidance to make sense of it, and a safe space to speak it without fear. That wish became a vision. And that vision became Nawiri.

Built for the generation coming up. Built for communities that have been waiting too long. Built on the belief that professional care and open, barrier-free access are not two separate things. They are two halves of the same commitment.
